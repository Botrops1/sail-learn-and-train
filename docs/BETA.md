# Quiet beta: a guide for sailors

## 1. What it is

**Sail: learn and train** is a free browser app that shows a complete beginner what every rope, sheet and sail of a cruising yacht does, on a 3D Hanse 508. It is made by one person (not a sailor yet) with an AI coding assistant, so **a real sailor's eye is exactly what it needs**.

Live site: https://botrops1.github.io/sail-learn-and-train/

## 2. Five things to try

Each link opens the live site in the right place. Try them on your phone first, then on a computer if you can.

1. **Points of sail**: https://botrops1.github.io/sail-learn-and-train/?v=1&m=exercise&ex=points-of-sail — tap **Watch it**. The app sails the exercise and stops after each step.
2. **Tacking**: https://botrops1.github.io/sail-learn-and-train/?v=1&m=exercise&ex=tacking — tap **Do it myself**.
3. **Setting the sails**: https://botrops1.github.io/sail-learn-and-train/?v=1&m=exercise&ex=set-sails — choose **Realistic** ropes on the card, then **Do it myself**.
4. **Leaving the berth**: https://botrops1.github.io/sail-learn-and-train/?v=1&m=exercise&ex=leave-berth — tap **Do it myself**. It starts in the marina.
5. **The Learn list**: https://botrops1.github.io/sail-learn-and-train/?v=1&m=learn — tap any part of the boat, or search for "vang".

## 3. What we want to hear

- **Anything that is wrong for a real yacht** (most important): a rope that does something it would not do, a name, a number, a sail that behaves wrongly. Say what you would see on a real boat.
- Anything confusing: a word, a step, a button you did not understand.
- Anything that does not work on your device.

The Russian text is an AI draft that nobody has checked. If you read Russian, tell us what is wrong.

## 4. How to report

- In the app: **Settings → Share** copies a link to exactly what you see. Paste it into your report.
- On GitHub (needs a free account): https://github.com/Botrops1/sail-learn-and-train/issues/new/choose
- Or send a message to the owner with the link.
