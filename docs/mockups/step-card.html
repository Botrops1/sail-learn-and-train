One step card

Mockup for M93 (plan only, nothing is built yet). The step line, the hint and the "Step done" card become one card under the numbers. The controls at the bottom stay as they are. Tap the card's top line to open or close the hint. Pick a moment of the exercise below.

What changes

  • Sailing a step: one or two lines ("3/4 · goal"). Tap to read How and the live ✓ / ○ targets. On a phone it folds again at each new step.
  • Step done, mistake, Now you: the same card changes colour (green, orange, blue) and shows its buttons. These moments cannot be folded, because the learner must act.
  • No more duplicates: the line no longer says "Step 2 done … press Resume" while a second card says "Step 2 done".
  • The time widget keeps its Pause / Resume / Carry on button; the card's buttons do the same, right under the explanation.
