Phase 8 panel mockup

The two layouts M51 and M52 will build (issue #100): tabs under the sheet's handle on a phone, and Mode and Settings chips on a computer. The 3D view is a real screenshot; the panel is drawn here by hand, in the app's own colours and sizes. Everything you can tap in the frames works.

Agree it in the planning PR: reply "mockup OK", or say what to change. Nothing is built until you agree.

Phone: tabs under the handle (M51)

  • Tap the grey stripe: the sheet goes low → half → full → low, as today. Dragging works too.
  • Tap a tab: it shows that tab's content (and raises a low sheet to half).
  • Controls holds every section except Conditions, Learn, Mode and Settings, in the mode's order. Ropes shows only the rope chips and the selected rope's slider (M48).
  • The mode chip and the gear leave the row over the 3D view (the tabs replace them); the camera chip and "!" stay.

Computer: Mode and Settings chips (M52)

  • The Mode and Settings sections leave the columns. Two chips sit at the bottom right of the 3D view, beside the camera chip: Mode: Easy ▾ and the gear.
  • Tap Mode: Easy ▾: the same mode list as the phone's Mode tab opens. Choose one and it closes; the chip shows the new name.
  • Tap the gear: the settings open in a card above it; × or a tap outside closes it.
  • The same chips also replace today's mode chip and gear on a tablet or narrow window.

On a phone the computer frame is shown at half size: swipe it sideways to see the columns.
